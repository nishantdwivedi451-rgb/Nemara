# Nemara — Three Creative Directions

Three genuinely different readings of one brand. Each is judged on: distinctiveness × fashion credibility × usability × conversion × storytelling.

---

## Direction A — "THE MARGIN"
*The designer's notebook, opened to the public.*

**Concept.** Pratima taught for 25 years. Teachers live in the margins — the notes, corrections and small encouragements written beside the work. Nemara's website is her notebook: the jewellery is the "work", and the margins are where the story lives. Ink annotations, arrows, measurements and crossed-out first ideas sit beside every piece.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Colour** | Paper ivory `#F6F1E8` as the page. Nemara Ink `#4A1F5C` used *only* as handwriting, rules and underlines — purple as the colour of the pen, not the wall. Champagne pencil `#C2AE86` for measurement marks. |
| **Type** | A high-contrast editorial serif for headlines; a workmanlike grotesk for UI; a real handwriting face for margin notes. |
| **Hero** | A full-bleed sketchbook spread. The headline is typeset; Pratima's handwritten note circles one word and redraws it. |
| **Navigation** | Tabs like notebook dividers on the edge of the page. |
| **Product grid** | Pieces "pinned" to the page with annotations — a design notebook, not a grid. |
| **Product page** | The page is split: piece on the left, the annotated margin on the right (idea → hand → making → moment). |
| **Artists** | Signed pages, the way a student signs work: name, place, technique, written by hand. |
| **Motion** | Ink drawing on: strokes write themselves, sketches resolve into finished jewellery. |
| **Mobile** | Margins collapse into swipeable "note" cards under each image. |
| **Feeling** | Intimate, intelligent, human. Risk: it can read as crafty or stationery-like and undersell fashion credibility. |

## Direction B — "THE GALLERY OF HANDS"
*An exhibition that happens to be shoppable.*

**Concept.** Each piece is an artwork with a maker. The site is a dim, aubergine gallery: pieces lit like exhibits, wall labels instead of product cards, artists billed as exhibiting artists. Collections are "exhibitions" with opening dates.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Colour** | Aubergine walls `#231029`, gallery-light ivory `#F3EDE4` on the work, brass label plates `#A88D5A`. |
| **Type** | A museum-label serif at small sizes; very large, thin display caps for exhibition titles. |
| **Hero** | A single piece under a slow-moving spotlight on a dark wall. |
| **Navigation** | A minimal floor plan: Rooms (collections), Artists, Visit (contact). |
| **Product grid** | One piece per "wall", horizontal scroll through the rooms. |
| **Product page** | Exhibit view: the piece large, a wall label, an audio-guide style story. |
| **Artists** | Artist retrospectives with portraits and bodies of work. |
| **Motion** | Light sweeps, slow fades, a camera dolly between rooms. |
| **Mobile** | Swipe from wall to wall, full screen. |
| **Feeling** | Reverent, cultured, dramatic. Risk: dark and slow. It drifts into the "black velvet" cliché, browsing many pieces is hard, conversion is low, and it feels heavy for a fashion jewellery price point. |

---

## Direction C — "MANY VERSIONS"
*A fashion magazine about one woman with many selves.*

**Concept.** The Nemara woman is never one person. The site is a monthly fashion magazine where every section is a "version" of her: the 9-to-5, the after-hours, the celebration, the everyday. It uses issue numbers, cover lines, big type, bold crops and split-screens.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Colour** | Ivory and warm white do most of the work. Deep plum `#3E1650` for cover type. Each "version" carries its own tint (lilac, dusk violet, festive aubergine, soft lavender). |
| **Type** | Oversized editorial serif with tight leading for cover lines; a clean sans for everything operational. |
| **Hero** | A magazine cover: masthead, cover lines and a cover image, with a rotating line for which version of her you're seeing today. |
| **Navigation** | Masthead style: centred wordmark, sparse items, and an issue number in the corner. |
| **Product grid** | Editorial pagination: products interrupted by full-bleed campaign spreads and pull-quotes. |
| **Product page** | A feature spread: gallery, then the story as a magazine article. |
| **Artists** | Profiles written like interviews. |
| **Motion** | Page-turn reveals, type sliding into place, crop-zoom transitions. |
| **Mobile** | Reads like a fashion app: full-bleed stories, horizontal rails, sticky bag. |
| **Feeling** | Confident, fashion-forward, commercial. Risk: magazines are a well-worn idea, so without a signature it could belong to any brand. |

---

## Selection — "THE ANNOTATED EDITION" (C as the chassis, A as the signature)

Neither A nor C wins alone:

- **C** brings fashion credibility and conversion: big campaigns, editorial pagination, an easy-to-scan shop and a familiar fashion-ecommerce rhythm.
- **A** brings what no other jewellery brand can claim: **the designer's hand in the margin**, taken directly from Pratima's 25 years as a teacher.
- **B** is rejected. It is beautiful but dark and slow, and it works against discovery and the brief's "operationally effortless".

**The idea: Nemara is a fashion magazine that its designer has written in.**
The pages are crisp editorial layouts (C). The margins carry Pratima's purple-ink annotations, sketches and the artist's signature (A). Purple appears as **ink**: in handwriting, underlines, the selected state and the logo. It is an atmosphere and a hand, not a background colour.

**Why this passes the Golden Rule (logo removed):** you would still recognise it. Purple handwritten margin notes beside editorial jewellery spreads, numbered "Edition" sections, sketches that resolve into finished pieces and the "Hand behind the piece" signature are Nemara-specific devices, not theme components.

### System summary
- **Colour:** Paper `#F7F3EC` · Warm white `#FCFAF6` · Ink (signature purple) `#4B1D5C` · Night plum `#21102A` · Violet `#6E3C8C` · Dusty lavender `#B9A4C8` · Lilac mist `#E9E0EE` · Champagne `#C8B184` · Graphite `#5A5260`.
- **Type:** *Instrument Serif* (display, editorial, used large) · *Instrument Sans* (UI, operational) · *Caveat* (Pratima's margin hand, used sparingly).
- **Logo:** A Didone-contrast wordmark drawn as custom paths, plus the **N-with-a-bead** mark: the N's hairline stem is "wearing" a single bead. It works as favicon, stamp, emboss and social icon.
- **Motion:** Ink-draw (sketch → jewellery), type rising line by line, image crop-reveal, hover image swap. Durations 300–900 ms with an `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.1,1)` ease, and nothing bounces.
- **Mobile:** Bottom tab bar (Shop · Try On · Stylist · Wishlist · Bag), horizontal rails, sticky Add to Bag, filter sheet.
